# Hidden Markov Models for Market Regime Detection

## Core Concept
In speech recognition, HMMs model sequences of sounds (observations) generated by hidden states (words/phonemes). For market microstructure, the analogy is direct:
- **Hidden state** = Market regime (trending, ranging, high volatility, low volatility)
- **Observations** = Price changes, order flow, volume patterns

## Key Algorithms

### Viterbi Algorithm
Finds the most likely sequence of hidden states given observations. Dynamic programming approach - computes best path incrementally.

### Baum-Welch Algorithm
Expectation-maximization for learning transition and emission probabilities from observed data. Used when we don't know the true regimes.

### Forward Algorithm
Computes probability of observing the sequence given the model - used for computing likelihood.

## Market Application
- Regime 1: Low volatility, mean-reverting (observation: small price changes)
- Regime 2: High volatility, trending (observation: large directional moves)
- Regime 3: High volume, informed trading (observation: volume spikes with price)
